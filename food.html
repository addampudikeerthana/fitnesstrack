Food Plan

Healthy Food Guidance

🌅 Breakfast

Oats, milk, fruits, idli or dosa with vegetables.

🍎 Morning Snack

Fruit, nuts, sprouts or yogurt.

🍛 Lunch

Rice or roti, dal, vegetables and curd.

🥜 Evening Snack

Fruit, roasted chana, nuts or sprouts.

🌙 Dinner

Roti, vegetables and dal.

💡 Healthy Tips

Eat a variety of foods and avoid skipping meals.
